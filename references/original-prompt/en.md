# XXD Shop 001 · Original brief (English reading translation)

> Reading translation only. The canonical runtime prompt is [zh-CN.md](zh-CN.md).

Premium commercial product key-visual photography.

First, determine the input:

If the user provides a real product photo, packaging shot or product reference, the original product is the highest-priority visual authority. Faithfully keep the product's shape, proportions, structure, packaging, brand marks, text placement, colour, material, texture and key identifying features. Do not restyle, redesign or substitute the product. Light, shadow, reflection, perspective and ambient colour may be rebuilt naturally for the new scene so the product truly belongs in the image rather than looking pasted in. If the original background is cluttered, the subject may be extracted naturally and a new space built around it.

If the user only provides a product name, category or creative proposition, build a plausible product form and visual world from the product's attributes, function, material, consumption context and brand character, without mechanically applying a fixed template.

The image grows outward from the product itself. The subject is clear, real and has weight; its surface texture, edges and material feel believably tactile. The product rests naturally on a base, surface or structural relationship that echoes its character, with a clear centre of gravity and contact shadow, avoiding any sense of floating or a templated display podium.

The foreground and surroundings contain only objects, ingredients, materials or details that genuinely explain the product, at a natural density. The background stays restrained: soft gradients, low-contrast contours, natural cast shadows, blurred forms, texture or a faintly present thematic image may build the space, so the environment feels like the lingering resonance of the product's character rather than a pile of theme elements.

Light has a clear direction but stays soft and natural. It shapes the product's volume, material, edges and weight; tonal transitions are delicate, highlights have air, shadows keep their layers, and the base has a real contact shadow. Let light truly act on the product instead of evenly illuminating the whole frame.

Colour extends from the product itself. Background, base, props and ambient colour echo one another naturally, allowing subtle shifts of warm and cool, light and dark, and material, without a mechanical colour formula.

If a title is needed, the large type must become one of the clearest visual cores besides the product: bold in scale, complete in letterform, clear in outline and easy to read, still recognisable at a glance when the image is reduced. Do not let texture, illustration or a busy background run through the main letterforms. Supporting text, English, numbering and notes recede naturally, forming a refined editorial hierarchy.

The whole is premium, quiet, real and airy — rich but not overfull, with elements drawing on one another rather than artificially stacked. Avoid product distortion, altered packaging text, lost brand elements, floating subjects, cheap display podiums, mechanical symmetry, meaningless decoration, flat even lighting, a background that steals the show, and a templated e-commerce look.

The final result is a mature, brand-level e-commerce main image, product poster or brand key visual.
